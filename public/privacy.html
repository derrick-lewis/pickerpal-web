PickerPal Privacy Policy

Effective August 25, 2026

PickerPal is built to keep your data on your device. Using the app to catalog your finds requires no account and sends us nothing. Optional features — signing in, backing up, sharing a find by link, and browsing the community Finds feed — do involve PickerPal's servers, and the sections below describe exactly what those hold.

Data stored on your device

Everything you create in PickerPal — stores, items, photos, prices, notes, and store maps — is stored only on your device. We cannot see it. Deleting the app deletes this data. You can export your photos to your Photos library at any time from Settings.

Location

With your permission, PickerPal uses your device's location to suggest the store you're standing in, to sort stores by distance, and to attach a location to stores you add. Location is processed on your device and is never transmitted to us. When you search for nearby places, your coordinates and search text are sent to the search provider (Apple Maps, or OpenStreetMap's Overpass service) to return results; PickerPal sends them nothing else.

Advertising

The free version of PickerPal shows banner ads served by Google AdMob. PickerPal requests non-personalized ads only. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect device information such as identifiers, IP address, and coarse ad-performance data, as described in Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob's data practices. PickerPal does not share your catalog, photos, or location history with advertisers. Purchasing the one-time "Remove ads" upgrade removes all advertising, after which no ad requests are made.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. PickerPal receives only a confirmation that the purchase succeeded — no payment details. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Analytics

PickerPal contains no analytics or tracking SDKs beyond the advertising described above.

Security and abuse prevention

When you create a PickerPal account or sign in, our servers record the IP address the request came from and your device's browser/app identification (the "user agent"), and keep them with your account and with each sign-in session. We use this only to keep the service safe: rate limiting, investigating abuse, and — if someone uploads content that violates our Terms of Service, such as sexual content involving minors — identifying and blocking the account responsible.

If we remove an account for that kind of violation, we first preserve a copy of the account's records and content in restricted internal storage, separate from the app, so it can be provided to law enforcement where the law requires it. That preserved copy is never served in the app or shared with anyone else. We also keep a record of the blocked email address, Google sign-in identifier, and IP addresses so the account cannot simply be recreated.

Children

PickerPal is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If PickerPal's data practices change (for example, if optional cloud sync is added), this policy will be updated here and the effective date revised before the change ships.
